## 3. Proposed AI Job Recommendation System

The team brainstormed a potential AI-powered job recommendation feature for job seekers.

### Proposed Functionality

- Introduce a dedicated page displaying personalized job recommendations.
- Use AI to evaluate job seekers' profiles, including their skills, education, experience, and preferences.
- Continuously analyze available job postings on CareerConnect and rank them based on compatibility with each job seeker.
- Provide AI-generated summaries of recommended positions.
- Allow job seekers to view full job postings and apply manually.
- Potentially introduce AI-assisted application autofilling to simplify the application process.

### Implementation Status

**Status:** Proposed – Pending Team Approval

This feature remains a proposal and requires further discussion and approval from the full team before implementation.

**Tentative Timeline:** Development may begin before or during Sprint 3, with completion targeted for Sprint 3, subject to feasibility and team approval.
