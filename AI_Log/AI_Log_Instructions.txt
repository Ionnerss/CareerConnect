CAREERCONNECT - AI USAGE LOG INSTRUCTIONS

PURPOSE
This folder records how each team member uses AI during the CareerConnect
project. Logs must show what AI contributed, how its output was checked,
and what was ultimately used, changed, or rejected.

1. FOLDER AND REPORT SETUP
- Create a subfolder inside AI_Log using your own name.
- Upload one PDF report per sprint to your personal subfolder.
- Suggested path: AI_Log/FirstName_LastName/Sprint1_AI_Log.pdf
- Include your full name and sprint number at the start of each report.
- You may keep an editable draft, but it does not replace the required PDF.

2. WHAT TO RECORD
Record every use of AI related to the project, including brainstorming,
research, requirements, planning, design, coding, code review, debugging,
testing, and documentation. Record interactions even when you reject the
suggestions. If AI influenced a decision or deliverable, log it.

3. HOW TO MAINTAIN YOUR LOG
- Add an entry for each AI-assisted activity. Recommended: write it promptly
  and keep entries in chronological order.
- Identify the AI tool used. Include its model if known.
- Provide a conversation link, or include the complete prompt and relevant
  AI response in an appendix. A summary alone is not sufficient evidence.
- Review and validate AI-generated content before using it. Describe the
  checks actually performed and their results; do not claim unperformed checks.
- Explain your final decision and any changes made to the AI output.
- Be prepared to explain your AI usage and validation during lab reviews.

4. ENTRY TEMPLATE
Copy the following template for each activity. Date and tool/model fields
are included for consistency alongside the assignment's required fields.

Entry number: [Number]
Date: [YYYY-MM-DD]
AI tool/model: [Tool name; model if known]
Task ID/Title: [Related task or issue ID, if available, and short title]
Purpose of AI Use: [Why you used AI]
Chat Link or Prompt/Response: [Conversation URL or appendix reference]
AI-Suggested Content: [Summary of what AI proposed or generated]
Validation: [How you checked the output and what you found]
Decision: [Accepted / Modified before use / Rejected; explain changes or rejection]
Reflection: [What you learned and whether AI was useful]
Responsible Person: [Your full name]

Appendix entry, if no conversation link is provided:
Activity reference: [Entry number]
Full prompt(s): [Paste the complete prompt(s)]
Relevant AI response(s): [Paste the relevant response(s)]

5. SPRINT 1 USER STORIES REQUIREMENT
Include an AI log entry for the required activity of asking AI to generate
10 user stories for CareerConnect. Preserve the full prompt or chat link
and the user stories returned by AI.

After reviewing those stories, the team must brainstorm additional original
stories and features. Document these separately under the exact heading
"Team-Generated User Stories and Features". Do not label AI-generated ideas
as team-generated or mix the two sources. Be ready to explain and justify
the team's original additions during the lab.

6. BEFORE UPLOADING YOUR SPRINT REPORT
Check that every AI activity is recorded, each entry includes evidence and
all required fields, and the PDF is in your personal subfolder. Ensure links
work and appendix references point to the correct prompts and responses.
